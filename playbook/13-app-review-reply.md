Thank you. Here is the information requested. A screen recording from an iPhone running the latest iOS is attached. It starts at launch and shows the full flow.

1. Screen recording: attached. There is no account, login, user-generated content or paid content.

2. Purpose and audience: Wisconsin Eats is a free guide to restaurants in Wisconsin. Its focus is the state's food traditions: 402 Friday fish fries (with the fish each one serves and the nights it fries), 211 supper clubs and 28 local frozen custard stands. Each was checked by hand in September 2026 against a 2025 or 2026 source: the restaurant's own website or menu, local news, or its Travel Wisconsin listing. It also lists 16,346 restaurants, cafés, taverns and bakeries statewide. It is for Wisconsin residents and visitors deciding where to eat, especially on Friday nights. Unlike general restaurant apps, it says who serves a fish fry, which fish and which nights, from sourced facts, with no ads, account or tracking.

3. How to use it (no login, no setup, no sample files needed):
- Home → Friday Fish Fry (or Supper Clubs, Frozen Custard, Wisconsin Icons, Oldest Places, Inspections, All Restaurants) → tap any place.
- On a place, tap "Ratings, hours & photos" to open Apple Maps' own place card through MapKit. Directions, Call and Website are below it.
- Search from the Home search box by name, town, street or fish ("perch green bay", "supper club dells").
- The Map tab shows each guide as pins. "My location" and the Nearest sort use location only if allowed, on the device.
- The heart saves a place on the device, under the Saved tab. The About tab lists the data sources, licenses and the independence statement.
- On iPad, a sidebar replaces the tabs (Home, each guide, Map, Saved, About); a place opens in the right-hand column.
- Location: if you're outside Wisconsin (as App Review usually is), the map says so and stays on Wisconsin, and distance sorting still works from where you are.
- "WI Eats" is the short Home Screen name of "Wisconsin Eats: Restaurants".

4. External services and data:
- Apple MapKit: maps, local search to find a place's Apple Maps listing, and Apple's place card for live hours, photos and ratings.
- Core Location: optional, on-device only, for distance sorting.
- Core Spotlight: on-device indexing so places appear in iPhone search.
- No backend, accounts, analytics, advertising, payments or AI services. The restaurant data is bundled in the app.
- Bundled data sources: Overture Maps Foundation open place data (CDLA Permissive 2.0, with Foursquare-sourced records under Apache 2.0 and AllThePlaces under CC0; license texts are in the app); City of Milwaukee Open Data (active food and tavern licenses); Public Health Madison & Dane County open data (licensed establishments and inspection results); James Beard Foundation award records (facts only); and our own research of restaurants' public websites, menus and local news.
- The website with the privacy policy and support page is static and hosted on GitHub Pages: https://wisconsin.eatsranked.com

5. Regional differences: none. The content covers Wisconsin; the app is offered in the United States and works the same everywhere.

6. Regulated or protected material: the app is not in a regulated industry and includes no protected third-party material. The data is open-licensed or public record, with attribution and licenses on the About tab. Ratings, hours and photos are shown only inside Apple's own place card through MapKit, under Apple's terms; the app stores no ratings or reviews. Dane County inspection grades are labeled in the app as our own summary of public records, not official grades. Restaurant names are factual references. Every website link was checked before release, and adult venues are excluded. The app is independent and not affiliated with any restaurant, agency or the James Beard Foundation.
