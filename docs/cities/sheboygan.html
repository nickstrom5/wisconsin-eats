Sheboygan icons

    Since 1939

    Sheboygan bakery open since 1939 that bakes Sheboygan hard rolls in a brick hearth oven using a sponge-and-dough method.

  2. Johnston's Bakery

    1227 Superior Ave, Sheboygan

    Since 1950

    Family-owned Sheboygan bakery, founded in 1950 and on Superior Ave since 1957, that calls itself the 'Home of the Sheboygan Hard Roll' and also runs a frozen-dough plant.

    johnstonsbakery.com

  3. Miesfeld's Triangle Market

    4811 Venture Dr, Sheboygan

    Since 1941

    Family meat market founded in Sheboygan in 1941, known for its Grand Champion bratwurst and more than 25 bratwurst flavors, which it also supplies to local restaurants and grocers.

    1644 Calumet Dr, Sheboygan

    Since 1953

    Family-run Sheboygan restaurant since 1953 that serves charcoal-grilled brats and steak sandwiches on hard rolls, using meat from Miesfeld's.
