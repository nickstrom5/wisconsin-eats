About this region

Our hand-checked lists have 75 places in Western Wisconsin; the towns with the most are La Crosse (9), Chippewa Falls (8) and Eau Claire (6). The oldest with a verified founding year is Willow River Saloon/Carbone's Pizzeria in Hudson (1869). Among the 54 fish fries here, the fish named most often is cod (29), then walleye (10).

How we define it: Western Wisconsin here means Polk, Barron, Rusk, St. Croix, Pierce, Dunn, Chippewa, Eau Claire, Pepin, Buffalo, Trempealeau, Jackson, Monroe, La Crosse, Vernon and Crawford counties; a place is in it when its map point falls inside one of those counties. City pages here: Eau Claire, La Crosse. Statewide guides: fish fry, supper clubs, custard.
