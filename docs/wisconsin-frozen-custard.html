Custard, not soft serve

Frozen custard is ice cream made with egg yolk and churned slowly with little air, so it comes out dense and is served fresh, often with a flavor of the day. It is a Milwaukee-area tradition, and many stands around the state post a flavor calendar. Soft-serve and hard ice cream shops are not on this list unless they make custard.

15 of the 28 stands have a founding year we could verify. The oldest: Gilles Frozen Custard in Milwaukee (1938); Leon's Frozen Custard in Milwaukee (1942); Leon's Frozen Custard in Oshkosh (1947); Gilles Frozen Custard in Fond du Lac (1949).

Each stand here was checked open against a 2025 or 2026 source, such as its own flavor calendar or menu, or a dated news story. Soft-serve shops that don't make custard aren't included, and neither are chains with five or more Wisconsin locations. Plenty of stands shut for the winter, so look up the hours first. Custard questions

What is the difference between frozen custard and ice cream?

Egg yolk. Under the federal standard for ice cream (21 CFR 135.110), ice cream has at least 10 percent milkfat, and one with at least 1.4 percent egg yolk solids by weight may be called frozen custard. Custard machines also tend to whip in less air, which is why custard is denser, and many stands serve it fresh from the machine rather than from a freezer case.

What is a flavor of the day?

Many stands make vanilla and chocolate every day plus one or two rotating flavors, and post the month's schedule on their site or social media. Check the stand's own calendar; flavors change.

What is the oldest custard stand in Wisconsin?

Of the 15 stands here with a verified founding year, the oldest is Gilles Frozen Custard in Milwaukee, founded in 1938. That answers for our list only; stands without a verified year may be older.
