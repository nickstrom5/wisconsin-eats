About Wisconsin Eats

Who's behind Wisconsin Eats, and where its lists come from

Wisconsin Eats (“WI Eats”) is Nicholas Soderstrom's independent app and website, a free guide to 16,346 Wisconsin restaurants. Its 402 Friday fish fries, 211 supper clubs and 28 custard stands were each checked by hand, and there's no account to make, no ads and no tracking.

Who makes it

Nicholas Soderstrom builds and maintains both the app and this website. Wisconsin Eats is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by the City of Milwaukee, Public Health Madison & Dane County, the James Beard Foundation, Apple, or any restaurant, team or chain.

How a place gets on the lists

Every fish fry, supper club, custard stand and icon on the lists was looked up by hand in September 2026. Each needed a 2025 or 2026 source, such as its own website or menu, a local news story or its Travel Wisconsin listing, and its street address was checked as well. The research keeps facts and a source link for each place, never review text or anyone's ratings, and a place it finds closed is taken off.

Everything else among the 16,346 comes from Overture Maps' open place data, Milwaukee's list of active food dealer and tavern licenses, and Public Health Madison & Dane County's list of licensed establishments. Map listings stay in only where that source proved reliable: tested against the Milwaukee and Dane County license lists, its high-confidence listings turned out to be a licensed business 75–79% of the time. Each website link is fetched and checked, and one that no longer leads to the place's own site is dropped.

The letter grades on Dane County places are ours, set on a curve from the county's routine inspections since January 2023: a summary of its results, not an official grade. No other Wisconsin county publishes inspections in bulk, so places elsewhere have no grade. Star ratings, reviews, hours and photos in the app all come live from Apple Maps' place card; Wisconsin Eats keeps none of them and sorts by none of them.

Updates

The restaurant data in this build dates from October 12, 2026, and the hand checks from September 2026. A new build follows when the research or the open data changes, and that's when corrections land. Restaurants open and close, so call ahead before a long drive.
