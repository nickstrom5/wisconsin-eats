What counts as a Wisconsin fish fry

A Friday fish fry is a plate of battered or breaded fish, usually cod, perch, walleye, haddock or bluegill, with coleslaw, rye bread and a choice of potato: fries, potato pancakes with applesauce, or German potato salad. Many taverns, supper clubs, bowling alleys and family restaurants serve it every Friday of the year, not just in Lent. Many also offer a broiled or baked choice and a fish sandwich.

This list only includes places that serve one regularly. Lent-only church and Legion fries are left out, because their dates change every year. Seasonal Northwoods places are in when they post their season. For Lent 2027, see the Lent fish fry page, with the Lent dates and the places that serve perch, walleye or bluegill or fry on other days too.

Fish fry questions

Why does Wisconsin have a fish fry on Friday?

It grew out of the Catholic custom of not eating meat on Fridays. Until 1966, church rules in the United States called for no meat on every Friday of the year; today the requirement covers Ash Wednesday and the Fridays of Lent, for Catholics 14 and older. Wisconsin has large Catholic communities, many with German and Polish roots, and fish from Lake Michigan, Lake Superior and inland lakes. Taverns and supper clubs kept the Friday fish fry going all year.

Cod, perch, walleye or bluegill: what's the difference?

Cod and haddock are ocean fish with large, mild, flaky fillets. Yellow perch, walleye and bluegill are freshwater fish: perch fillets are small and sweet and usually come several to a plate, walleye fillets are thicker, and bluegill is a small panfish. Of the places in this guide that list their fish, 214 serve cod, 151 perch, 123 walleye and 49 bluegill. The perch, walleye and bluegill lists are on the Lent page.

Is the fish fry only on Friday?

Mostly. 37 of the 402 places here list their fish fry on at least one other day as well, most often Wednesday. They are listed together on the Lent fish fry page.

What comes with a fish fry?

Of the 267 places here that list their sides, 212 mention coleslaw, 91 rye bread and 49 potato pancakes. Fries, a baked potato and potato salad are the other common choices.
