Door County fish fries, supper clubs & more

6 fish fries, 5 supper clubs, 5 fish boils and 1 custard stand in Door County, each checked open in September 2026 against a 2025 or 2026 source.

About this region

Our hand-checked lists have 20 places in Door County; the towns with the most are Baileys Harbor (4), Fish Creek (4) and Sister Bay (3). The oldest with a verified founding year is White Gull Inn in Fish Creek (1896). Among the 6 fish fries here, the fish named most often is perch (6), then walleye and whitefish (4 each).

How we define it: Door County here means Door County; a place is in it when its map point falls inside the county line. Statewide guides: fish fry, supper clubs, custard.

Fish boils in Door County

5 places. The Door County-style fish boil: whitefish, potatoes and onions boiled outdoors over a wood fire, usually finished with a boil-over.

  1. The Log Den

    6626 WI-42, Egg Harbor

    Fish boil Since 2007

    Egg Harbor restaurant that hosts a Friday night fish boil during the warm-weather season, which opened May 15 in 2026.

    thelogden.com

  2. The Old Post Office Restaurant

    10040 N Water St, Ephraim

    Fish boil Sides: potatoes, onions

    Restaurant in Ephraim's restored early-1900s post office on Eagle Harbor with open-fire whitefish boils nightly except Sunday from mid-May through late October.

    oldpostoffice-doorcounty.com

  3. Kettle Black Fish Boil

    4158 Main St, Fish Creek

    Fish boil Sides: potatoes, onions, corn on the cob, cornbread · Since 2022

    Waterfront Fish Creek fish boil with Tuesday through Saturday seatings at 5:30 and 7:30 that cooks boneless whitefish in a seasoned broth with potatoes, corn, andouille and vegetables.

    kettleblackfishboil.com

  4. Pelletier's Restaurant and Fish Boil

    4199 Main St, Fish Creek

    Fish boil Sides: potatoes, coleslaw, rye bread, cherry pie · Since 1970

    Founders Square restaurant in Fish Creek with nightly Lake Michigan whitefish boil-overs at 5, 6 and 7 pm, plus 8 pm in peak season.

    pelletiersfishboil.com

  5. White Gull Inn

    4225 Main St, Fish Creek

    Fish boil Sides: potatoes, coleslaw, bread, cherry pie · Since 1896

    Historic Fish Creek inn serving Lake Michigan whitefish boils Wednesday, Friday, Saturday and Sunday evenings May through October and Friday evenings the rest of the year.

    whitegullinn.com

Icons and old favorites in Door County

7 places. Long-running and honored places that aren't in the lists above.

  1. AC Tap

    9322 WI-57, Baileys Harbor

    Since 1933

    Northern Door County roadside tavern on Hwy 57 opened by Al Ohnesorg in 1933 and named for postwar owners Art Smith and Carl Becker, known for pub fare and dice games under a Pabst sign.

    actap.net

  2. Sway Brewing + Blending

    2434 County F, Baileys Harbor

    Since 2024

    Baileys Harbor brewery, bakery and kitchen making beers from Midwestern ingredients; a 2026 JBF beverage-program finalist.

    swaybeer.com

  3. Wilson's Restaurant & Ice Cream Parlor

    9990 Water St S, Ephraim

    Since 1906

    Seasonal Ephraim soda fountain and burger restaurant open since 1906, known for ice cream sundaes and home-brewed draft root beer.

    wilsonsicecream.com

  4. Al Johnson’s Swedish Restaurant & Butik

    10698 N Bay Shore Dr, Sister Bay

    Since 1949

    Family-run Swedish restaurant opened in 1949 in Sister Bay, known for the goats that graze its Norwegian-built sod roof each summer.

    aljohnsons.com

  5. Cultured

    2570 County Q, Sister Bay

    Since 2023

    Door County sourdough bakery in Sister Bay known for breads, bagels and focaccia; a 2026 Outstanding Bakery finalist.

    culturedsourdough.com

  6. Renard's Cheese

    2189 County Rd Dk, Sturgeon Bay

    Since 1961

    Four-generation family cheesemaker founded in 1961 when Howard and Angela Renard bought Algoma's Rosewood Dairy, now selling fresh squeaky curds and 100-plus cheeses at its Sturgeon Bay store.

  7. Nelsen's Hall Bitters Pub

    1201 Main Rd, Washington Island

    Since 1899

    Washington Island dance hall built in 1899 whose tavern stayed open through Prohibition by selling Angostura bitters, which visitors still drink to join its Bitters Club.
