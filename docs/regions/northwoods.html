About this region

Our hand-checked lists have 53 places in the Northwoods; the towns with the most are Hayward (11), Eagle River (5) and Land O' Lakes (4). The oldest with a verified founding year is Bent's Camp Resort in Land O' Lakes (1896). Among the 37 fish fries here, the fish named most often is cod (11), then walleye (9).

How we define it: The Northwoods here means Oneida, Vilas, Forest, Florence, Marinette, Iron, Price, Ashland, Sawyer, Bayfield, Douglas, Washburn and Burnett counties; a place is in it when its map point falls inside one of those counties. Statewide guides: fish fry, supper clubs, custard.

Icons and old favorites in the Northwoods

  1. Bodin Fisheries

    208 Wilson Ave, Bayfield

    301 Rittenhouse Ave, Bayfield

    Since 1973

    Bayfield inn in an 1890 Queen Anne Victorian home opened by Mary and Jerry Phillips in 1973, whose Landmark Restaurant serves regional, seasonal dinners.

  4. West's Hayward Dairy

    15848 W 2nd St, Hayward

    Since 1951

    Downtown Hayward dairy bar making small-batch homemade ice cream on site since 1951, typically offering about 40 flavors.

    1710 Marinette Ave, Marinette
