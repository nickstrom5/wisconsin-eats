Built around Friday night, not star ratings

Most restaurant apps sort by stars. This one starts from the Wisconsin calendar: fish on Friday, a supper club when there's something to celebrate, custard on a summer evening, and the taverns and bakeries that have been open for generations. The 402 fish fries, 211 supper clubs and 28 custard stands in the guides were each checked open in September 2026, and so was every place shown with a founding year or an honor. Ratings, hours and photos are Apple Maps' own, in its live place card.

Fish fry and app questions

Is there a fish fry outside Lent?

Yes. The places in our fish fry guide serve a regular fish fry, not a Lent-only one (a few Northwoods spots are seasonal), and 37 of the 402 fry on days besides Friday too. Church and Legion fries that run only in Lent aren't listed. Lent 2027 begins on Ash Wednesday, February 10, 2027.

Does it cover the Northwoods and Door County?

Yes. The Northwoods page has 53 hand-checked places across 13 northern counties, and the Door County page has 20, fish boils included. Western and Central Wisconsin have their own pages, and the app's search works statewide.

How were the fish fries and supper clubs checked?

By hand, in September 2026. Each fish fry, supper club and custard stand on our lists had to turn up in a 2025 or 2026 source, such as its own menu page, a dated news story or its Travel Wisconsin listing. The other places among the 16,346 come from Overture Maps' open place data and the City of Milwaukee and Dane County license lists.

Do you rate the restaurants?

Not with stars or reviews, and no list here is sorted by anyone's ratings. The one grade we give is the Dane County inspection summary, which is ours and not official. Open a place and Apple Maps' own card shows its ratings, hours, photos and directions, live from Apple.

Why do only Madison-area places have inspection grades?

Public Health Madison & Dane County publishes its routine inspections as open data, so Dane County places get a grade from their results since January 2023, set on a curve. The grade is our own summary, not an official one. Other Wisconsin counties don't publish inspections in bulk.

Does the app track where I go?

No. If you allow location, your iPhone or iPad uses it to list the closest places first, and it never reaches us. Skip it and the guides, search and map work the same.

A place on your list closed, or one is missing. Who do I tell?

Send the name and town to nick@eatsranked.com; we correct the list in the next update.

Is there an Android app?

Not for now: the app is for iPhone and iPad. On an Android phone, the web app at wisconsin.eatsranked.com/explore/ has the same guides. Asking for Android at nick@eatsranked.com helps move it up the list.
