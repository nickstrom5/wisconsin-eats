# wi-eats

Two things built from one pipeline:

1. **Wisconsin Eats**, a free iPhone and iPad app (App Store name "Wisconsin Eats: Restaurants", subtitle "Fish Fry & Supper Club Guide"), and its website `docs/` for wisconsineats.com. See "The app" below.
2. **The web leaderboard** of every restaurant in Wisconsin, published as a private claude.ai Artifact from `site/index.html` (its JSON is git-ignored because it carries Google 2021-derived ratings; rebuild it with the pipeline). It reads `site/wisconsin.json` (every place, columnar), then lazily loads `site/wi_shapes.json` (state and county outlines, the first time the Map opens) and `site/wi_detail.json` (honors text, license numbers and inspection breakdowns, in idle time or when a drawer opens).

Built separately from `chi-eats/` (the Chicago/Illinois version), reusing its structure, UX and rules. Nothing here reads chi-eats at run time: `data/chain_auv.json` is a copy of its chain sales list, and the sales model's exponent (0.764) is the one fit on Chicago's published sales.

## The app

| Path | What |
|---|---|
| `project.yml` | XcodeGen spec: app `WisconsinEats` (bundle `com.wisconsineats.ios`, iOS 18, iPhone + iPad), tests `WisconsinEatsTests` |
| `WisconsinEats/` | SwiftUI app. Guides (Friday Fish Fry, Supper Clubs, Frozen Custard, Wisconsin Icons, Oldest Places, Dane County Inspections, Near Me, All), search by name/town/street/dish, filters, a clustered map, saved places, About with sources and licenses. iPhone: tabs; iPad: split view |
| `WisconsinEats/Resources/places.json` | The whole data set, bundled (about 3.8 MB), copied from `data/app/places.json` |
| `WisconsinEatsTests/` | Search, guides, sorting, filters, saved places, inspection and match-rate copy, the bundled file |
| `docs/` | The website (https://nickstrom5.github.io/wisconsin-eats/, GitHub Pages; a state subdomain of the hub domain later): landing page, three statewide guides, 13 city pages, privacy, terms, 404, sitemap. Generated by `scripts/make-site.py` |
| `playbook/` | Strategy, naming, App Store listing (ASO), App Store Connect, site and email runbook, sources, launch checklist |
| `scripts/` | `make-brand.swift` (icon, og.png, favicons), `make-site.py` (the website), `capture-screenshots.sh` (App Store shots) |

What the app does **not** contain: any Google-derived data (ratings, reviews, price levels, review mentions). Each place opens Apple Maps' own place card (`mapItemDetailSheet`) for live ratings, hours and photos. There is no backend, no account, no analytics and no ads. Location only sorts lists on the device. Core Spotlight indexes the hand-checked places.

```bash
brew install xcodegen
xcodegen generate && open WisconsinEats.xcodeproj
cd pipeline && WI_APP=1 ../.venv/bin/python wisconsin.py && cd .. && cp data/app/places.json WisconsinEats/Resources/   # refresh the data
.venv/bin/python scripts/make-site.py                                                                              # refresh the website
```

What's in a guide:
- **Fish fry, supper club and custard lists** are hand-checked. Each place matches an open entry in `data/curated.json` or `data/research/app/*.json` with a 2025–2026 source: its own site or menu, a dated news story or its Travel Wisconsin listing. Records hold facts only: the fish, days, sides, founding year and a one-line note in our words.
- **Everything else** is licensed data:
  - Overture Maps places. In app mode a listing stays if it's on a license list, hand-checked, a Meta listing with confidence of at least 0.9, or from AllThePlaces or DAC.
  - The Milwaukee license list.
  - The Dane County license list.
- **Confidence tiers** replace the Google-based check, calibrated against the two license lists (`data/wi/calibration_app.json`). Meta listings with confidence of 0.95 or more matched a licensed business 79% of the time in Milwaukee and 75% in Dane County. Listings from 0.90 to 0.95 matched 38% and 48%. BrightQuery and lower-confidence Meta listings matched 22–31%, so they are dropped.

## Rebuild

```bash
python3 -m venv .venv && .venv/bin/pip install pandas pyarrow rapidfuzz requests shapely duckdb playwright
.venv/bin/python pipeline/fetch_overture.py     # Overture places + state/county outlines (lakes cut out) -> data/wi (about a minute)
.venv/bin/python pipeline/fetch_extras.py       # Milwaukee/Dane outlines for calibration, Dane County address points
curl -o data/meta-Wisconsin.json.gz https://mcauleylab.ucsd.edu/public_datasets/gdrive/googlelocal/meta-Wisconsin.json.gz
curl -o data/review-Wisconsin.json.gz https://mcauleylab.ucsd.edu/public_datasets/gdrive/googlelocal/review-Wisconsin.json.gz   # 780 MB
bash data/raw/official/_scripts/download_all.sh  # Milwaukee licenses, Madison & Dane County establishments/inspections/violations
cd pipeline
../.venv/bin/python reviews.py      # fish fry / curds / custard / supper club mentions in 10.2M reviews -> data/wi/review_signals.parquet
../.venv/bin/python listings.py     # clean Overture listings, match Google 2021 -> data/wi/stage1.pkl
../.venv/bin/python calibrate.py    # match rates against the official lists -> data/wi/calibration.json
../.venv/bin/python wisconsin.py    # keep rule, license lists, dedupe, brands, tags, honors, inspections, estimates -> site/*.json
```

`fetch_extras.py` writes `data/wi/addresses_dane.parquet` (Overture address points, used to place Dane County license records, which have no coordinates) and `data/wi/calib_areas.json` (Milwaukee city limits and the Dane County outline). `WI_SITE=/some/dir` writes the site JSON elsewhere for a side-by-side test.

## Inputs

| File | What | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `data/wi/overture_wi_*.parquet`, `wi_shapes.json` | Places statewide, their source datasets; state and county outlines | Overture Maps 2026-09-23.0 (places, divisions, base/water) |
| `data/raw/official/milwaukee/*` | Active restaurant, retail food and tavern licenses, with coordinates | City of Milwaukee (ArcGIS), refreshed nightly |
| `data/raw/official/madison_dane/*` | Licensed establishments, inspections 2012–Jul 2026, violation sheets 2021+ | Public Health Madison & Dane County (maps.cityofmadison.com OPEN_DB_TABLES) |
| `data/meta-Wisconsin.json.gz`, `review-Wisconsin.json.gz` | Ratings, reviews, price level; review text (Sep 2021 snapshot) | UCSD Google Local Data 2021 |
| `data/curated.json` | James Beard, America's Classics, supper club and other verified icons | Hand-verified research, Sep 2026 |
| `data/chain_auv.json`, `chain_auv_wi_extra.json` | Chain average sales per store | QSR 50 2026, Technomic, company filings, franchise disclosures |

There is no statewide list: Wisconsin DATCP publishes no bulk file of licensed restaurants or their inspections (its search site is one record at a time and blocks automated access), and the other county health departments use HealthSpace or the state site, neither with an export. See `data/raw/official/SOURCES.md`.

## Calibration (calibrate.py)

Inside Milwaukee's city limits and Dane County every Overture eating/drinking listing was checked against the active license lists (same business name nearby, or the same street address with a distinctive name word in common). Share that matched a licensed restaurant, tavern or retail food business:

| Listing | Milwaukee | Dane County | Chicago (for reference) | Here |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Meta + open in Google 2021 | 81% | 76% | 74% | kept, "two sources" |
| Meta only | 42% | 47% | 40% | kept, "listing only" |
| Chain store feed + Google 2021 (AllThePlaces/DAC) | 60% (of 5) | 85% (of 13) | — | kept, "two sources" |
| Chain store feed only | 24% | 28% | — | kept, "listing only" (chain stores) |
| Foursquare / BrightQuery / Microsoft + Google 2021 | 22% | 19% | 5–14% (single source) | dropped |
| Foursquare / BrightQuery / Microsoft only | 11–24% | 7–23% | 5–14% | dropped |
| Google 2021 says closed | 7% | 2% | 2% | dropped |

Hand-checked institutions (`data/curated.json`) are kept whatever their source, and ones verified closed (`data/research/closed.json`) are removed.

The other direction: the kept map listings held 59% of Milwaukee's licensed restaurants/taverns and 65% of Dane County's. The rest are added from the license lists (food trucks and caterers at shared kitchens, 5+ license-only places at one address, are hidden with the non-restaurants). These rates are lower bounds: a real place licensed under a different name counts as a miss.

## Cleanup rules (each one came from a QA finding here or in Illinois)

- **Dropped:** names that say closed, retired or coming soon (and their twins nearby); bare numbers; names that are a street address, a town, "Village of X", a lone word like "Kitchen", or have no Latin letters; non-Wisconsin zips; non-food businesses; places outside the state line (Lakes Michigan and Superior cut out) or 30 km from the rest of their town; listings with no street, no zip and no Google match; places our research verified as closed (`data/research/closed.json`).
- **Kept whatever the source:** hand-verified open institutions (`data/curated.json`). The ones the map data lacks as a place to eat (inns, a bowling-alley supper club, shops) are placed with their Google listing; ratings come along only when Google files them as a restaurant.
- **Towns:** one spelling per town (St./Saint → St., Mt → Mount, Ft → Fort, "City of X" → X, DeForest, De Pere, Fond du Lac, La Crosse, McFarland…), the same function for map listings, license records and address points; same-name towns in far-apart counties are split ("Scott (Burnett Co.)").
- **Addresses:** Wisconsin grid numbers (W3176, N44W33013), numbered roads in one spelling (US-51 = US Highway 51, WI-113 = State Highway 113, CTH M = County Rd M), ranges and "4943&4947"; directions typed into the address ("Located in: …", "Between X & Y") are cut to the street address.
- **Google matches:** host venues (malls, hotels, grocery, campgrounds, bowling alleys, cheese shops…) never lend their reviews; a match needs a shared word that isn't a town, cuisine or generic word (MKE, Hut, Wok…); one Google listing per place, best pairs first.
- **License matches:** same name within 150 m, or the same street number and street with a distinctive word in common (spelling slips allowed). A second pass after geocoding merges a license into a map listing within 60 m with a matching name, or at the same address when the record has no location; a listing that already has its own license only takes a same-name second license. Inspections come from the matched license itself (a hotel's license isn't its bar's).
- **Brands and chains:** a brand needs the listing's own name to match the brand rule, or the chain's own store feed ("Golden Chicken" labeled Golden Chick, "The Caribou Tavern", "Cava Bakehouse" aren't the chains). Unbranded places form a chain when they share a real website and a distinctive name word (Café Hollander ×4, MACS ×8); directories and store websites don't count. Chains take one cuisine.
- **Cuisine:** a specific word in the name first (Pelmeni, Bar-B-Q, Belgian…), then the listing's category, then "bar"-type names, then Google's categories. Places re-filed as supper clubs take the supper clubs' usual price.
- **Non-restaurants** (hidden unless "include non-restaurants" is on), checked on the cleaned display name: gas-station and grocery counters, delivery-only brands (MrBeast Burger, Wow Bao…), cheese, wine, candy and meat shops, nutrition clubs; contract and corporate cafeterias (Davians, Guckenheimer, Exact Sciences…), airports, stadiums, State Fair and Summerfest grounds; words like school, church, hotel or club only when the place isn't plainly a bar, café or restaurant (by name, category or Google's category) and has under 200 reviews; license-only records for apartments, pools, sportsmen's clubs, caterers, and food trucks at shared kitchens (5+ license-only places at one address).
- **Duplicates:** same name within 80 m, same brand within 150 m, same brand/name at the same street number, a chain copy with no address near one with an address, the same unbranded name within 500 m in one town, a one-source copy within 1.5 km of its confirmed listing, and two listings on one license.
- **Names:** repaired encoding (Windows-1252 mojibake), HTML entities, emoji; "X LLC - Y" → Y when X is short; corporate suffixes; marketing tails after " - "; notes in parentheses; ", WI" tails; Chinese/Japanese/Korean duplicates of an English name; store numbers and town tails on chains; all-caps license names re-cased.
- **Wisconsin tags:** supper club = named one, 5+ reviews (and 4%+ of reviews) calling it one, or on the verified list; fish fry / cheese curds / custard = 3+ reviews mentioning it (custard also for Culver's, Kopp's, Freddy's). The fish fry, custard and curds boards rank the average star rating of just those reviews, shrunk toward the statewide average by 5 reviews' worth.
- **Inspections (Dane County only):** routine inspections since Jan 2023; score = 100 − (40 × share needing re-inspection + 3 × violations/visit + 8 × risk-factor items/visit + 25 × pest share + 8 × last visit needed re-inspection + 15 × any suspension), shrunk toward the county mean; A–F graded on a curve in Chicago's proportions. Repeat violations are the sheets' "CHECKED" marks; pesticide items aren't pests.
- **Map:** the drawn outline also cuts the big inland lakes (Winnebago, Mendota, Petenwell…) and slivers along the shore; county lines are clipped to land; the gold-ringed top 25 always stay in the zoomed view.
