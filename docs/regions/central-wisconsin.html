About this region

Our hand-checked lists have 42 places in Central Wisconsin; the towns with the most are Stevens Point (10), Wausau (4) and Wisconsin Rapids (4). The oldest with a verified founding year is Mint Cafe in Wausau (1888). Among the 39 fish fries here, the fish named most often is haddock (22), then perch (18).

How we define it: Central Wisconsin here means Marathon, Portage, Wood, Lincoln, Langlade, Clark and Taylor counties; a place is in it when its map point falls inside one of those counties. Statewide guides: fish fry, supper clubs, custard.
